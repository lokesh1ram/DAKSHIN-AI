📌 1. Project Overview & Scope

ActiveLife Wellness Assistant AI is an anthropometric health evaluation and monitoring platform designed for modern wellness centers and personal health tracking. The system extracts and computes 14 anthropometric biomarkers (BMI, Visceral Fat, Body Fat %, Muscle Mass, Body Water %, BMR, Skeletal Muscle, Trunk Subcutaneous Fat, and Metabolic Age) and generates clinical PDF reports.

The platform features Dakshin AI, an interactive smart voice robot assistant trained in the style of Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, "OK Google", and Gemini. Dakshin speaks aloud in three languages (English, Telugu, Hindi) and answers critical customer doubts regarding lifestyle habit transformations:

  • Daily Step Count: BMI-tailored target of 8,000–10,000 daily steps to mobilize intra-abdominal fat.
  • Food Habits: Fiber-first meal protocol, 25–30g clean protein per meal, zero refined white sugar and deep-fried snacks, and an 8:00 PM dinner deadline.
  • Water Intake: Daily hydration protocol of 3.5 to 4.0 liters with 500ml warm water upon waking.
  • Good Sleep: 7 to 8 hours of deep restorative sleep by 10:30 PM to suppress nocturnal cortisol and visceral belly fat storage.
  • Reduce Visceral Fat: Actionable strategies to safely normalize organ fat levels below 9.

const handleWeightChange = (val: string) => {
  setWeight(val);
  const w = parseFloat(val);
  const h = parseFloat(height);
  if (w > 0 && h > 0) {
    const calcBmi = (w / ((h / 100) * (h / 100))).toFixed(1);
    setBmi(calcBmi);
  }

📐 6. Anthropometric Mathematical Formulations

Biomarker Formula Clinical Classification
Body Mass Index (BMI) BMI = Weight(kg) / (Height(m))² Normal: 18.5–24.9 | Overweight: 25.0–29.9 | Obese Class I: 30.0–34.9 | Obese Class II: ≥ 35.0
Visceral Fat Bioelectrical Impedance / DEXA index (1 to 30) Healthy: 1–9 | Borderline: 10–14 | Cardiovascular Risk: ≥ 15
Body Water % Total Intracellular + Extracellular fluid % Male: 50%–65% | Female: 45%–60%
Basal Metabolic Rate Harris-Benedict equation based on gender, age & weight Target daily baseline calorie burn required for homeostasis
